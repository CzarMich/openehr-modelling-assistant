    1. Get started
    
                        
      
                            
    1. 
                                Open Chat and sign in with your workspace account. If you need an
                                account or a password reset, contact your workspace administrator. The
                                Sign in button at the top right is available whenever you are signed
                                out.
                            
      2. 
                            
    2. 
                                Open My AI connections. Choose Connect Codex and
                                follow the sign-in instructions, or enter your Anthropic API key for Claude. Your
                                workspace login and AI connection are separate.
                            
      3. 
                            
    3. 
                                Choose your Assistant, describe what you want to model, and select
                                Send message. Include who the model is for, what information it should
                                capture, and any source documents.
                            
      4. 
                            
    4. 
                                Answer the assistant's questions and review its sources and draft. Use
                                New conversation for a different topic or assistant.
                            
      5. 
                        
    
                        
    
                            Try: “Help me plan a kidney infection assessment template. First identify the information we
                            need, find reusable archetypes, and list any unanswered questions.”
                        
    
                        
    
                            When the assistant offers choices, click one answer, or tick several boxes and press
                            Use selected options. Choose Write another answer for your
                            own wording, or Skip question to continue without choosing. A moving
                            progress ring shows when work is in progress; it pauses when your answer is needed. On
                            phones, use Chat settings to open connections, repositories and sharing.
                        
    
                        
    
                            Connections and conversations belong to your profile. Repository files follow the access
                            rules of the repository where you save them.
                        
    
                        
    Organise your chats
    
                        
    
                            Select Create project in the sidebar to group chats in a private folder.
                            Open a project's heading to see its conversations, or select + Chat to
                            start one inside it. Use the arrow beside a chat to move it to a project. You can rename a
                            project; deleting it keeps its conversations in Unfiled chats. On phones,
                            select Toggle conversations to see these controls. Each chat keeps its own
                            files and chosen repository.
                        
    
                        
    
                            Select × (Delete conversation) beside a chat to delete its messages and
                            uploaded files. You will be asked to confirm. Other chats and the project are kept.
                        
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        
    2. Upload documents
    
                        
      
                            
    1. 
                                In Chat, select the + button (Add files and images)
                                inside the message box. You can also drop files onto the box or paste a PNG/JPG image
                                while writing. You can select several PDFs and spreadsheets together.
                            
      2. 
                            
    2. 
                                Each file appears as a card. Text ready means text was extracted;
                                Image ready means the assistant can view the image.
                                Partly read or Needs attention includes an
                                explanation. You can keep typing while files upload.
                            
      3. 
                            
    3. 
                                Ask the assistant to read the files, summarise the requirements, and use them to prepare
                                a draft. Ask it to identify missing or conflicting information before modelling.
                            
      4. 
                        
    
                        
    
                            Text PDFs, Excel and OpenDocument spreadsheets, Word documents (DOCX), CSV, JSON, XML and
                            plain text can be read. Other formats can be attached, but may need conversion before their
                            contents can be used. PNG and JPG images can be interpreted by your selected assistant. For
                            a scanned PDF, attach the relevant pages as PNG/JPG images or provide a text version.
                        
    
                        
    
                            Select an image thumbnail to preview it, or × to remove an attachment before sending. Sent
                            files appear beside your message and remain available under
                            Files in this chat, where you can remove them from future responses. Use a
                            clear crop for small print. Image interpretation can miss details; check extracted clinical
                            information against the original before using it in a model.
                        
    
                        
    
                            PNG/JPG images must be single-frame and no larger than 20 megapixels. A
                            prepared copy up to 2048 pixels per side is sent to the assistant; the original is kept.
                        
    
                        
    
                            Each file can be up to 10 MiB. A conversation can hold
                            10 files, up to 30 MiB in total. Very long documents may
                            be shortened during extraction. Select a filename to download the original, or use its
                            remove button to detach it.
                        
    
                        
    
                            Only upload information you are allowed to use with your chosen AI provider. Attached images
                            and source excerpts may be sent to that provider when you send a message.
                        
    
                        
    
                            After attaching a publication, try: “Read this publication. Extract the assessment findings,
                            tests and follow-up information it describes. Show the sources and uncertainties, then
                            propose reusable archetypes and a draft template.”
                        
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        
    3. Add sources and repositories
    
                        
    
                            Open My sources and repositories in Chat. Give each connection a name you
                            recognise. Your personal connections are available only to your profile.
                        
    
                        
    Add a CKM
    
                        
    
                            A CKM is a library of reusable archetypes and templates. Choose CKM, enter
                            its HTTPS service address, and select Save connection. Ask the CKM owner
                            for the service address and an access token if required. If your organisation already
                            provides the same CKM, it stays available and no duplicate is added.
                        
    
                        
    Add your Git repository
    
                        
      
                            
    1. Choose GitHub repository or GitLab repository.
      2. 
                            
    2. 
                                Enter the repository's HTTPS URL, such as
                                https://github.com/your-name/your-models, and the target branch, such as
                                main.
                            
      3. 
                            
    3. 
                                To save files, enter a personal access token with write access to that repository.
                                Public repositories can be read without a token. Your organisation may need to approve
                                access.
                            
      4. 
                            
    4. 
                                Select Save connection, then choose it under
                                Save artifacts to and press
                                Save repository selection for this conversation. Wait for
                                Active and check its URL and branch.
                            
      5. 
                        
    
                        
    
                            You need permission to use the repository and branch. For an existing repository, choose a
                            branch that already exists. Removing a personal connection does not delete files from the
                            repository.
                        
    
                        
    
                            For GitHub saves, create a fine-grained personal access token limited to your repository
                            with Contents: Read and write. Paste it only in the connection form. To add
                            or replace a token later, select Update access beside the connection, enter
                            the token and select Save connection. Your active selection is kept.
                        
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        
    4. Save your work
    
                        
      
                            
    1. 
                                Check Save artifacts to. Keep Enterprise repository to
                                use your organisation's destination, or select your personal repository. Press
                                Save repository selection after changing it, then check the active URL
                                and branch shown below it.
                            
      2. 
                            
    2. 
                                Ask the assistant to prepare and save the draft. For a personal repository, include a
                                relative file path, such as templates/assessment.oet.
                            
      3. 
                            
    3. 
                                Read the proposed destination, file and changes in the confirmation. Select
                                Confirm save when they are correct, or cancel.
                            
      4. 
                        
    
                        
    
                            A save only happens after confirmation. If someone has changed the file, ask the assistant
                            to read the latest version and prepare the change again. Saving a model keeps it as a draft;
                            it does not approve it for clinical use.
                        
    
                        
    
                            If the assistant reports no personal repository, select your connection in this
                            conversation, press Save repository selection and retry. If another tab
                            changed the destination, your unsent instructions are kept so you can check the current
                            choice before sending again.
                        
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        
    5. Find and review models
    
                        
    
                            Open Models, choose an enterprise project, and select a file to
                            inspect its source. Use Download source for a copy, or
                            Discuss in chat to ask about that version. To explore a personal
                            repository, ask the assistant in Chat.
                        
    
                        
    
                            Open Governance to inspect enterprise model reviews and validation
                            results. The actions you can take depend on your assigned role. Check the exact version and
                            evidence before recording a decision. Generated models need qualified review before clinical
                            use.
                        
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        
    6. Share a chat
    
                        
      
                            
    1. Open a conversation and select Share chat.
      2. 
                            
    2. 
                                Review the messages for information you do not want to share, then select
                                Create link and copy the link.
                            
      3. 
                            
    3. 
                                Send it to someone who can sign in to this workspace. The link shows a read-only
                                snapshot of the current messages for 7 days.
                            
      4. 
                        
    
                        
    
                            Later messages and uploaded original files are excluded. Text from an upload may still
                            appear in the shared messages. Return to Share chat and select
                            Revoke link to stop access early. Creating a new link replaces the previous
                            one.
                        
    
                    
    
                    
    
                        
    Connect Microsoft Copilot Studio
    
                        
    
                            Use this workspace's modelling tools from your Copilot Studio agent. Your Microsoft account
                            and organisation must allow MCP connections.
                        
    
                        
      
                            
    1. 
                                Open your agent in
                                Copilot Studio. Go to Tools → Add a tool → New tool → Model Context Protocol.
                            
      2. 
                            
    2. 
                                Name the connection openEHR Modelling. Describe it as tools for finding
                                archetypes, drafting models and checking modelling artefacts.
                            
      3. 
                            
    3. 
                                Paste the server address below. Choose API key, then
                                Header, and enter the header name below.
                            
      4. 
                            
    4. 
                                Create the connection, enter the workspace connection key supplied by your
                                administrator, and add it to your agent.
                            
      5. 
                            
    5. 
                                Ask your agent to list the CKM sources. Confirm it uses ckm_sources and
                                returns a real result before using it for modelling.
                            
      6. 
                        
    
                        
    
                            
                            
                            
                            
    
                                
    
                                    Administrators can access the configured workspace key here. It grants this
                                    deployment's modelling access; keep it private.
                                
    
                                
                                
    
                                    
                                    
                                    
                                
    
                            
    
                            
    
                        
    
                        
    
                            This connects tools inside Copilot Studio. Your browser chats continue to use the assistant
                            selected in Chat. Microsoft tenant policies and connection approval still apply.

    7. Solve a problem
    
                        
    
                            
    I cannot send a message.
    
                            
    
                                Check that you are signed in and your selected assistant is connected under
                                My AI connections. If chat is unavailable for this workspace, contact
                                your administrator.
                            
    
                            
    My file says unsupported or failed.
    
                            
    
                                Read the message beside it. Try a text-based PDF, DOCX, CSV or plain-text version. For a
                                scanned publication, attach the relevant pages as PNG/JPG images or extract its text
                                first. Reattach images uploaded before image support was available. Split files that
                                exceed the size limit.
                            
    
                            
    My repository refuses a save.
    
                            
    
                                Check the repository URL, branch and token permissions. If the token has expired, remove
                                the connection and add it again with a current token. Protected branches may require a
                                different branch. Your administrator must also enable saving for this workspace.
                            
    
                            
    A shared link does not open.
    
                            
    
                                Sign in to the same workspace. If the link has expired or been revoked, ask the owner
                                for a new link.
                            
    
                            
    I use Microsoft Copilot Studio.
    
                            
    
                                Your organisation can connect its Copilot Studio agent to the modelling server. Ask your
                                administrator for access and setup details. The Assistant selector in this browser
                                supports Codex and Claude.
                            
    
                        
    
                        
    
                            If you need help from your administrator, share the error message and what you were trying
                            to do. Keep passwords and access tokens private.
